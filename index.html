Charbonnance privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 · version 1

Who is responsible

Charbonnance is made by Charley Guillot, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. For any question or request about your data, write to guillotcharley@gmail.com.

What we store

Your account: your email address, a display name and your password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider).

Your training: the routines you write, the workouts you log (exercises, sets, reps, weights, times, distances, notes, pauses and timer scores), the personal bests worked out from them, and any exercises you create.

When you accepted this policy and which version.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health records, and we ask for no information about your body.

Why, and on what basis

We use this data only to provide the app: to keep your account, your plans and your history, and to show your progress. The legal basis is the performance of the contract you enter into by creating an account (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Who processes it

Your data is stored by Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in Zurich, Switzerland, a country the European Commission recognises as giving adequate protection. Supabase acts only on our instructions under a data processing agreement; as a US company it relies on the EU standard contractual clauses for any access from outside Europe.

Reminders such as the rest timer and the "workout still running" alert are scheduled on your device and never leave it.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted at once and cannot be recovered. Copies may persist in our provider's backups for a short period until those backups expire.

Your rights

You can see and correct your account details in the app (Today → Account), export all your data as a file (Account → Export my data) and delete your account and all its data (Account → Delete account).

You can also ask, by writing to guillotcharley@gmail.com, for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a copy in a portable format, or to restrict or object to its processing. If you no longer have the app, you can ask for your account to be deleted the same way, from the address you signed up with. We answer within one month.

If you think your data is mishandled, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority, or to the authority of the country where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app tells you and asks you to read and accept the new version.

Questions or requests: guillotcharley@gmail.com
